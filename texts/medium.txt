Medium mode:

This mode is a bit challenging, here you will be having a life bar, and piranha fishes. If you catch piranha fishes, you will lose a life. You need to catch fishes in the objective within 2 minutes.
Scoring System
Golden Fish = 15 points, Objective Fishes = 2 points
Other fishes = -1 points (if target not fulfilled)
if target fulfilled, +1 points for all normal fishes
